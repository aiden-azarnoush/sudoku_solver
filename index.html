Sudoku Solver

Enter the clues, press Solve. Backtracking with constraint propagation, in your browser.
Click a cell, then tap a key here or type on your keyboard. Arrow keys move between cells; Backspace clears.
Read from a picture
click to choose a screenshot or photo of a Sudoku, or drag one here
Works best with a clear, straight-on screenshot. The digits it reads go into Edit mode so you can check them before solving.
Clues are black; the solution appears in green. A puzzle needs at least 17 clues to have a unique solution.
